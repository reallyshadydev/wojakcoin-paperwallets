Security Best Practices

Follow these guidelines to keep your WojakCoin safe.

Go Offline

For maximum security, disconnect from the internet before generating wallets. You can save this page and run it offline.

Protect Your Private Key

Never share your private key (WIF) with anyone. Anyone with your private key can spend your WojakCoin.

Use a Secure Printer

Use a printer that is not connected to the internet. Avoid wireless or network printers when printing private keys.

Store Safely

Keep your paper wallet in a secure, dry location. Consider making multiple copies and storing them in different places.

Laminate for Durability

Consider laminating your paper wallet to protect it from water damage, fading, and wear over time.

Verify the Source

This generator is open source. Review the code to confirm no data is transmitted externally. All operations happen in your browser.

How To Use

A simple guide to creating and using your WojakCoin paper wallet.

1

Generate

Click "Generate New Wallet" to create a unique WojakCoin address and private key pair using cryptographically secure randomness.

2

Print

Click "Print Paper Wallet" to print the wallet. Use a local, non-networked printer for best security. Fold along the line to hide the private key.

3

Fund

Send WJK to the public address shown on the left side of your paper wallet. Anyone can send coins to this address.

4

Redeem

To spend your WJK, import the private key (WIF) into a WojakCoin wallet application. After importing, transfer funds to a new address.
